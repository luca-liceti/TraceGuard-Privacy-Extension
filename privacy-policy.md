# TraceGuard Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 3, 2026

TraceGuard analyzes the pages you visit locally in your browser to provide privacy and website-safety scores. It stores analysis results, including domain names, timestamps, detector results, score history, settings, and PII **field types** that you choose to enter. It never reads, stores, or transmits the contents of form fields, passwords, cookie values, or other entered personal data.

TraceGuard sends only a base domain to ToS;DR (`api.tosdr.org`) to retrieve publicly available privacy-policy ratings. On the update schedule selected in Settings, it also downloads public tracker and cookie-list updates from their published sources (`raw.githubusercontent.com` and `easylist.to`). Those update requests contain no browsing, account, or other user data. It does not use analytics, telemetry, advertising SDKs, or sell or share personal data. Analysis data is stored locally in Chrome extension storage; sensitive history is encrypted after the user unlocks the vault.

You can review, export, or clear locally stored data from TraceGuard Settings. Contact the publisher through the Chrome Web Store listing for privacy questions.
