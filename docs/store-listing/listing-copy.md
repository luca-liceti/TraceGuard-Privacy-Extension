## 3. Detailed description (long description)

```
TraceGuard gives every website a transparent, real-time privacy score so you can see how your data is treated before you share it.

Unlike silent blockers, TraceGuard shows you exactly what a site is doing with your information through two clear metrics:

- Website Safety Score (WSS): a 0-100 rating for every site based on reputation, tracking, cookies, input fields, and privacy-policy strength.
- User Privacy Score (UPS): a personal behavioral score that reflects your privacy health over time.

FEATURES

- Real-time tracker detection: identifies third-party trackers using a curated list plus bundled, credible databases (DuckDuckGo Tracker Radar, EasyPrivacy, Disconnect).
- PII monitoring: detects sensitive form fields (passwords, emails, cards) and warns you before you submit data to low-trust sites.
- Cookie auditing: detects tracking cookies from Set-Cookie headers and the DOM. Only cookie names and metadata are read, never values.
- Policy grading: bundled ToS;DR ratings by default, with an optional and off-by-default live lookup for unrated sites.
- Modern dashboard: an interactive control center with your privacy score, rankings, and settings, available as a side panel and a full dashboard.
- Threat protection: an on-device phishing and malware blocklist that updates through signed, verified feeds.

PRIVACY BY DESIGN

- Everything runs locally on your device. No accounts, no telemetry, no backend.
- Sensitive history is encrypted behind an optional master password (your "vault").
- TraceGuard never reads, stores, or transmits the contents of forms, passwords, or entered data.
- The only optional external request is Enhanced Policy Analysis (off by default), which sends just the domain of unrated sites to ToS;DR.

Local-first and transparent: the same privacy you expect, applied to the tool that protects you.
```
